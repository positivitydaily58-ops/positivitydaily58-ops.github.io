Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 October 2026. This policy covers clipper, the private upload tool of the Positivity Daily YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

The app asks for one Google permission: youtube.upload. It uses it only to upload videos that the channel owner selects, with their title and description, to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, change or delete other data.

Where data is stored

The OAuth token stays on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database. It does not sell or share any data, and it does not use data for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Remove access

Remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Then delete the token file on the computer.
